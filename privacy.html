Privacy Policy

egg-potato uploader is a private tool used only by the owners of the Egg and Potato Couple YouTube channel to schedule uploads of their own videos.

  • It reads video files from the owners' own Google Drive folder and uploads them to the owners' own YouTube channel.
  • It does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and it is not offered to other users.
  • Access tokens are stored only as encrypted secrets in the owners' private automation settings.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
